<h2>🧩 What I’m Building Right Now — Cortix</h2>

<p>
Cortix is where most of my current engineering energy goes. I’m designing and implementing:
</p>

<ul>
  <li><b>Backend architecture</b> with clear service boundaries and predictable failure behavior</li>
  <li><b>Async job processing</b> using queues and workers for long-running and CPU-heavy tasks</li>
  <li><b>Database schemas & migrations</b> with a strong focus on data integrity and future evolution</li>
  <li><b>Idempotent APIs</b> to handle retries, partial failures, and network issues</li>
  <li><b>Operational concerns</b>: retries, timeouts, backoff strategies, and observability</li>
  <li><b>Dockerized deployments</b> with reproducible local and production environments</li>
</ul>

<p>
Cortix is intentionally backend-heavy. I care less about UI polish at this stage and more about making sure the core systems are boring, stable, and easy to reason about.
</p>
